THE STARTING COMPARISON

Keep the learner’s work constant.

“An AI system offers the same kind of assistance, and I undertake the same intellectual work.”

The question compares the source of assistance. The activity of the person receiving it remains the same.

TWO PATHS THROUGH ONE QUESTION

Separate the source from the work.

The paragraph opens into two paths. A human and an AI can supply a suggestion; the learner’s response is a further part of the account.

The distinction makes the comparison inspectable. It does not establish that every actual interaction is equivalent.

A CONDITION CHANGES

A correct caution can miss the question.

A THEOLOGICAL DISTINCTION ENTERS

What if feedback helps create an idea?

The AI offers a hypothetical question: “Does your argument distinguish methodological naturalism from metaphysical naturalism?”
