What can be examined

The record makes it possible to examine the reasons for a redirection, to revisit an inference, and to compare what “understanding” means in different passages. The full text provides context for those readings.

Evidence of a learner’s development requires interpretation and further inquiry. This single-author study offers a research prototype for theological pedagogy. It does not establish a general learning effect or assign shares of authorship.
